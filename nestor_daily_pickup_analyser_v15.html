Nestor daily pickup and cancellation control — v15

What this does — load your Apaleo export, pick a date window, then work three tabs:
· Pickup — bookings created in the window (your new business for the day/days), with wrong-rate-plan checks.
· Cancellations — bookings cancelled in the window, each checked against its cancellation-policy terms: Within terms (no charge) vs Outside terms → charge due. Totals the revenue to recover.
· All — the full file.
New in v15 — opens on a Dashboard: click any property to see its bookings split by channel, by month or by stay date, each checked against budget ADR. Booking lists are grouped by property in a compact, readable layout — hit “Show full detail” whenever you need every Apaleo column. The v14 date fix and CSV export are unchanged.
Distribution: CARLYSLE (CAR) & THE ANTIGALLICAN (DAG) use the NEW distribution (OTALOS / OTASEMILOS); all others use the OLD matrix — LOW (Oct–Apr) / HIGH (May–Sep) × plan × unit tier (Studio·1BD / 2BD / 3BD+4BD). Flex 24H=1d, Flex 3D=3d, SemiFlex 7D=7d, SemiFlex 14D=14d, NONREF=always chargeable.
